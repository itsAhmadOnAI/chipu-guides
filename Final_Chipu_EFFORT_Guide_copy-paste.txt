CHIPU · EFFORT GUIDE (copy-paste version)
Source: code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config (Adjust effort level) and code.claude.com/docs/en/costs

STEP 1: small job? Lower the effort. Inside Claude Code type:
/effort low

Saved as your default for later sessions. For this session only: type /effort, pick low, press s (v2.1.257+).
Go back:
/effort medium
One session only, from the terminal:
claude --effort low

STEP 2: hard job? Add this word anywhere in ONE prompt:
ultrathink why do the login tests fail?

Claude reasons deeper on that turn only; your session effort stays the same.
In the terminal the word shows in rainbow colours when it raises the turn to xhigh (v2.1.311+).

Levels: low, medium, high, xhigh, max. Default on Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5 = medium.
If CLAUDE_CODE_EFFORT_LEVEL is set, it overrides /effort.
